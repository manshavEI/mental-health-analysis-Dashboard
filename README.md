# mental-health-analysis-Dashboard


This dashboard provides a quick overview of **mental well-being indicators** (anxiety, depression, sleep, stress, and workload) along with **mood trends across age groups** and **physical activity patterns by gender**, enabling easy comparison and insights at a glance.
**Tech Stack (for this dashboard):**

* **Frontend / Visualization:** Power BI (DAX, Power Query)
* **Data Processing:** Excel / CSV dataset (cleaned & transformed in Power Query)
* **Analytics:** DAX measures for averages, counts, and aggregations
* **Design & UI:** Power BI dark theme, custom visuals (cards, line chart, donut chart)

**source**: kaggle
Data on mental health indicators across different age groups and genders, including anxiety, depression, sleep quality, stress, workload, mood ratings, and physical activity patterns, visualized for comparative analysis and insights.


**Features / Highlights**
**Business Problem**
Mental well-being data is often scattered and difficult to interpret, making it hard for educators, organizations, and researchers to identify patterns related to stress, mood, sleep, and workload across different age groups and genders.
**Goal of the Dashboard**
To provide a centralized, visual overview of key mental health indicators, enabling quick comparison, trend identification, and data-driven insights for better decision-making and awareness.
KPI Cards: Display average scores of anxiety, depression, sleep quality, stress, and workload for quick assessment.
Mood Rating by Age (Line Chart): Shows how mood levels vary across different age groups.
Gender Filter: Allows users to analyze mental health metrics based on gender.
Physical Activity Distribution (Donut Chart): Highlights the relationship between gender and physical activity duration.

**Business Impact & Insights**
Helps identify age groups with higher stress or lower mood levels.
Supports targeted wellness initiatives and mental health interventions.
Enables stakeholders to monitor overall well-being trends at a glance.
